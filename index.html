About

A bit about me

I'm a software engineer working on the integrations team, where I spend most of my time turning inconsistent third-party APIs into pipelines that finance and HR teams never have to think about — accounting platforms, payroll systems, payment processors, that sort of thing.

What I enjoy most is the architecture problem underneath every integration: building something generic enough to onboard the next system in weeks rather than months, without it collapsing into a pile of special cases.

Outside of work I'm usually deep in a personal coding project, a video game, or a DIY job around the house. Most days involve several walks with my two puppies, and I try to get out on the bike or travel somewhere new when I can.
